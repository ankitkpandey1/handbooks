### Applicant Notice: Use of ResumeRank v3 in Hiring

[Employer Name] uses an artificial-intelligence-assisted tool called ResumeRank v3 during some hiring processes. The tool produces a score based on information processed from an applicant’s submitted materials. A recruiter reviews the score and may override it; the score is not necessarily the final hiring decision.

We are reviewing and will provide additional information about:

- the data used to develop and evaluate the tool;
- the information considered when generating scores, including whether protected characteristics or related information are used;
- the tool’s validation and performance results;
- how long applicant information and scores are retained;
- whether vendors or other parties can access the information;
- what explanations are available to applicants;
- how applicants may request review, correction, or reconsideration; and
- applicable legal and compliance safeguards.

For questions or to request information about how ResumeRank v3 was used in your application, contact [contact name/email]. Until these details are confirmed, the employer should not publish statements claiming that the tool is unbiased, compliant, validated, or free from protected-attribute use.
