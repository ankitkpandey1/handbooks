# Frozen tasks for E01

## T1 — Research

A materials scientist has one week to decide which of 400 papers and 30 plausible mechanisms justify three expensive experiments on why a new electrolyte loses conductivity after cycling. Evidence is heterogeneous and some papers reuse the same datasets.

## T2 — Strategy

A small B2B software company must decide whether to launch a free tier while two better-funded competitors can respond. It has six weeks, limited sales data, and can run only two reversible market tests.

## T3 — Weak verifier

A university dean asks one analyst to publish a policy for student use of generative AI next semester. Evidence about learning effects is weak and fast-changing; faculty values conflict; enforcement can harm students.

For each task, a successful architecture must produce a concrete first 48-hour operating plan, identify machine-scale work, specify what can reject outputs, cap human review, and state a stopping or authority boundary.
