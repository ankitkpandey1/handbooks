An expert community is considering a reward intervention intended to preserve cooperation. In a symmetric two-strategy game, cooperation/cooperation pays R, cooperator versus defector pays S, defector versus cooperator pays T, and defection/defection pays P. After the intervention, plausible but not empirically calibrated ranges are R in [2.5,3.5], S in [-0.5,1.5], T in [2.5,4.5], and P in [0.5,1.5]. Across these possible games and starting populations, does cooperation reliably survive, and what measurement is most decision-relevant? Do not treat the ranges as empirical probabilities or invent observed outcomes.
