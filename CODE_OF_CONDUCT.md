# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Be civil and argue about substance. Technical disagreement is the point of this repository;
contempt is not.

Specifically:

- Attack claims, not people. "This claim is unsupported because…" is welcome. "You clearly
  don't understand…" is not.
- Bring evidence. A challenge to a claim is only useful with a reason attached.
- Assume the other party is competent and acting in good faith until shown otherwise.
- Accept correction without drama, including from people less senior than you and from
  agents.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption,
publishing others' private information.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and manuscript content in
this repository.

## Enforcement

Reports go to the maintainer via a GitHub issue, or privately through the email on the
maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit,
close, or delete contributions, and may block accounts, at their discretion.

There is one maintainer, so response times are best-effort rather than guaranteed.
